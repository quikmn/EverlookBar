EverlookDB Bar: info bars and a clean minimap for WoW Forever.

### Bars
As many as you like: along the top, bottom, left or right edge, or anywhere. One row or more, the whole edge or a set length, any background colour and opacity, font, size and spacing. Plugins sit in left, centre and right groups.

### Plugins
Every LibDataBroker plugin from any addon can go on a bar. Built in, each one switchable:

- XP bar: thin bar with rested XP and percentage.
- Reputation bar: your watched faction, coloured by standing.
- Professions: your skill in each one; click to open, coloured when it's time for the next rank at a trainer.
- Performance: FPS and latency; memory and CPU per addon in the tooltip.
- Location: zone, subzone and coordinates.
- Durability: your most worn item; every slot in the tooltip.
- Mail, Bags (profession bags apart), Quest log (38/45), Gold with this session's change, Calendar with invites, Clock.

### Clean minimap
Drag the minimap itself, resize with the mouse wheel, square or round, any border. Hide or move the zone text, clock, tracking and more. Every addon's minimap button goes into one small drawer. If another addon already manages the minimap, EverlookDB Bar leaves it alone.

### Using it
/ebar opens the settings window (also under Settings > AddOns). Drag plugins between bars on the Plugins page. /ebar unlock lets you drag bars, the minimap and plugins anywhere.
